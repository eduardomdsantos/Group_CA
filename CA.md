## Historial Development
### Timeline
2003-2005 Profile and Personalization Era
MySpace was created on August 2003 by Tom Anderson, Chris DeWolfe, and Aber Whitcomb as a project with eUniverse (known as Intermix Media). It offered profile customization and a platform for independent bands.
The Facebook.com launched at Harvard University in February 2004 and it initiated a cleaner and more structured approach to social networking
In February 2005, three former PayPal employees registered YouTube which transformed the internet from a text and photo medium into a hub for user generated videos.

2006 - 2008 Change of Guard and Microblogging
In March 2006, Twitter entered the scene with a 140 character limit and popularized real time microblogging and worldwide news delivery
In November 2006, Google bought YouTube for 1.65 billion dollars after recognising the growth potential
In 2008, Facebook surpassed MySpace in monthly active users and offically claimed market dominance

2010 - 2012
In October 2010, Kevin Systrom and Mike Krieger launched Instagram as an IOS only app, which leveraged the upgraded canera if the iPhone 4 to focus fully on filtered photography and immediate liking mechanics
In July 2011 , Evan Spiegel and Bobby Murphy launched Snapchat. They redefined social habits among younger people through disappearing photos and messages
In April 2012, Facebook bought a fast growing Instragram for 1 billion dollars.

2016 - Present
In 2016 ByteDance launched Douyin in China which was followed up by TikTok in 2017. Tiktok disrupted traditonal social networks by using hyoer accurate content recommendation algorithm instead of a user's friend graph
In 2020-2021, Youtube Shorts and Instagram Reels were introducted to compete with Tiktok's vertical video interface.
Between the years 2022-2024, Elon Musk ended up buying Twitter for 44 million dollars and rebranded it to X. In between these years Facebook also made history by becoming the first social networking platform to surpass 3 billion monthly active users

2023 - Present Artificial Intelligence Era
Artificial Intelligance (AI) has changed social media through personalised recommendations, chatbots and targeted advertisement. Platforms that we have looked at already like Tiktok, Instagram and Facebook use AI to suggest content based on user's interests. However, AI has also increased concerns about privacy and fake information. Nowadays it still continues to shape how people communicate online.

## Societal Viewpoint Analysis
### Definition of the societal point of view:
A societal viewpoint is a perspective of society or an average person on a topic. In case of this case study, it will examine how the growth and evolution of social media affected multiple social areas such as: Education, quality of life in communities and politics.

### The impact:

## Education:
How it is related: As the social media became more accessible and widely used, a lot of people from the education sphere have adapted the social media platforms for various reasons, the impact of that adaptation is shown below: 

# Positive: 
1. Stay in touch: Students have easier time communicating and socializing  with each other via shared platforms like Instagram, tiktok and snapchat 

2. Information: It became much easier for students to find information by themselves and study indepnedatly. "YouTube hosts extensive instructional video content that can support education or aid in completing complex tasks" (Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development, 2026).

# Negative: 
1. Distraction: It has become a common issue that social media platforms, distract students from their work, weather it is at home or even in class, where they choose social media and entertainment over a lecture or a class (Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development, 2026).

2. Focus: There is a connection between long exposure to the format of short vidos that don't require concentration and future problems with focusing on long mundaine tasks. "A significant negative relationship between MPSVATQ and theta power index reflecting the executive control in the prefrontal region" (Frontiers of Neuro Science, 2024).

## Communities

How it is related: The development of social media has led to huge changes in how people communicate and interact with each other on daily basis, examples of these changes are shown below:

# Positive:
1. Easier connections: With the new, widely used social media platforms it has become much easier to find new people around you to socialize with. According to DataReportal.com, almost 6 (5.81) billion people in 2026 meet and connect via social media rather than searching for people in person. The research also shows that the rate of this virtual connections has grown by about 2.7 % since 2025.

2. Worldwide communication: Obviously, the main point of social media is to allow people to connect or stay in touch regardless of the distance between them. Social media allows two people that live in completely different sides of the world communicate with minimal delay, as if they were standing right beside each other.

# Negative:
1. Social isolation: Social media makes communication easier, but excessive use of it can cause loneliness and lead to social isolation. "Problematic social media use shows a moderate and consistent association with greater loneliness" (Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development, 2026).

2. Mental health strain: As mentioned before, the development of social media has lead to easier communications and connections, which also means it has lead to easier spread of bullying. WHO states that around 1 in 6 teenagers have experienced cyberbullying (World Health Organization, 2024).

# Politics

How it is related: Although the social media in its largest is used for entertainment, it also serves as a useful tool in political side of many countries. The more developed and popular social media platforms in general become, the more they are used in political sphere 

# Positive:
1. Easier to share opinions: Many social media platforms have turned into fields of discussion of thoughts. Social media platforms where hundreds of people can meet and interact create a great opportunity for all sorts of discussions, especialy on sharp and important topics such as politics (Pew Research Center, 2025).

2. Promotion of viewpoints: Social media platforms make it easier to share  your opinions on political topics or the other way around, receive opinions and thoughts of other people on a certain political topic. "Half of U.S. adults social media users say social media is very pr somewhat important for them when it comes to finding others who share their view abut important issues" (Pew Research Center, 2025).

# Negative:
1. More disinformation: This point explains itself, it is an average thing for political opponents to post disinformation about each other, even bigger problem is that people will very rarely double check the information they consume. Anything they see will usually be accepted regardless of weather it is truth or not

2. Affected decision-makeing: Social media platforms like facebook, instagram and twitter can contribute to creation of so called echo chambers. An echo chamber is an environment in which one article, opinion or point of view is quickly spread and grown popular. People that find themselves within an echo chamber can have their original opinion either reinforced or completely changed, causing a real problem with forming a truly unaffected opinion (Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 2021)


## Business Viewpoint Analysis

### Defining the Business Viewpoint
The business viewpoint looks at money, competition, and value. When we look at social media, we all have the same question: how are they making money when we use it for free? However, the answer is that they are making money from people's time and attention, mostly by showing adverts.

### MySpace
MySpace was one of the first big social media sites. In 2005, a company called News Corporation bought MySpace for $580 million (NPR, 2011).

Positive:
1. It shows that social media is worth a lot of money (NPR, 2011).
2. At its peak, it had 70 million visitors per month (NPR, 2011).

Negative:
1. When Facebook became more popular, a lot of people switched to it, and advertisers moved there too.
2. MySpace was sold for only $35 million (NPR, 2011).

From a business perspective, this shows that having a lot of viewers alone is not enough; you need to keep improving your app and follow trends, or people will move on.

### Facebook
Facebook is free to use, but it makes almost all of its money from ads. When people use Facebook, they share information such as their age and what they like so Facebook can show ads to the right people.

Positive:
1. In 2024, Meta made $164.5 billion, and $160.6 billion of that came from ads (Meta Platforms, 2025).
2. By December 2024, around 3.35 billion people were using Meta's apps every day (Meta Platforms, 2025).

Negative:
1. About 98% of its money comes from ads.
2. Ad prices can go down too; in 2023, the average price per ad went down by 9% (Meta Platforms, 2025).

From a business perspective, this shows that user information is very valuable, but relying on only one way to make money is risky.

### Instagram
Facebook agreed to buy Instagram for about $1 billion (NPR, 2012). At that time, Instagram was a small company with 13 employees, but it was growing quickly.

Positive:
1. Facebook got rid of the competition.
2. Later, Instagram became one of Meta's most important ways to make money, and it helped Meta compete with new apps like TikTok (Yahoo Finance, 2025).

Negative:
1. $1 billion was a lot of money for a company with only 13 employees (NPR, 2012).
2. In 2018, the two people who made Instagram left Facebook because they did not agree with Mark Zuckerberg (MacRumors, 2018).

From a business viewpoint, buying a competitor can be a smart way to stay on top. However, it costs a lot of money, and you can lose the people who made the app good.

### TikTok
TikTok belongs to ByteDance, a company from China. It became popular very quickly because of its short videos and its "For You" page, which shows each person videos they would like.

Positive:
1. In 2025, it generated about $33.1 billion in ad revenue (Revenue Memo, 2025).
2. It does not rely only on ads. People can buy gifts and send them to creators on live streams. This brought in over $3 billion in 2025 (Revenue Memo, 2025).

Negative:
1. The US is TikTok's biggest market, so almost $12 billion in ad money was at risk when the US wanted to ban it (Search Engine Land, 2025).
2. ByteDance had to give control of TikTok in the US to a new company owned mostly by American investors, and ByteDance kept only 19.9% (TheJournal.ie, 2026).

From a business perspective, this shows that a new idea like short videos can beat older apps very quickly. However, politics can be a big risk for a global business.
